We perform noninvasive and minimally invasive tests and procedures that check, support, and improve your heart health. We’ll work with you to develop a personalized treatment plan and treat you with the most effective medications. Our services include:

 

  • Evaluating you for coronary heart disease, heart-valve disease, and heart defects

Welcome to the Nephrology Department!

We are here to provide specialized care for your kidney health. Our team of dedicated healthcare professionals offers a range of services designed to support you through every stage of kidney disease and treatment. Here’s what we can do for you:

Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) Management: If you have been diagnosed with CKD, we will work closely with you to manage and slow the progression of the disease. Our team will create a personalized care plan that includes lifestyle recommendations, medications, and regular monitoring.

Dialysis and Dialysis Referrals: For those who need dialysis, we provide comprehensive support and guidance. We offer dialysis treatment and can also refer you to specialized dialysis centers if needed, making sure you receive the best possible care.

Transplant Work-Up and Referrals: If a kidney transplant is a viable option for you, we will assist you through every step of the process. This includes conducting necessary evaluations and tests and referring you to experienced transplant centers to ensure smooth and coordinated care.

Education: Understanding your condition is crucial for effective management. We offer educational resources and sessions to help you and your loved ones learn about kidney disease, treatment options, and how to maintain a healthy lifestyle.

Our goal is to provide you with empathetic, comprehensive care to help you achieve the best possible outcomes. Feel free to reach out to us with any questions or to schedule an appointment.

Your kidneys are our priority, and we're here to support you every step of the way.
