Prepare for your visit

Visitors seeking assistance from the Public Contact Team at the Providence Regional Office are encouraged to schedule a virtual or in-person appointment on the Visitor Engagement Reporting Application (VERA). VERA is a self-service portal which allows visitors to schedule, reschedule, and cancel appointments.

Please follow this link to schedule the appointment. You will receive email confirmation of your appointment.

You are also welcome to call us directly at or email pctc.vbapro@va.gov for assistance. 

Parking at the Providence VA Regional Office can be a bit challenging due to its central location, but there are options available. 

  • Direct Parking Lots: There are three parking lots immediately adjacent to the building; one in front, one on the side, and one behind.
  • Street Parking:  Metered parking is available along Westminster Street and surrounding streets. Be sure to check the hours and rates for metered spaces.
  • Garage Parking: Several nearby garages are available, including Westminster Street Garage, Union Station Garage, and Th Industrial National Bank Building Garage, all within a short walk of the office.  

When arriving, it's always a good idea to leave extra time for parking, especially during busy hours. If you're unsure of the best option, calling the Providence VA Regional Office directly at may provide more detailed and up-to-date information. 

The nearest public transportation option is the Rhode Island Public Transit Authority (RIPTA) bus service. The closest bus stop is located at Westminster Street, just a short walk from the office. We are also within walking distance of a Kennedy Plaza. 

Visit the official RIPTA website for schedules, routes, and additional information.

 

When you visit, please bring a current, unexpired photo ID. Depending on the reason for your visit, you may need to bring additional these documents and information about you and your dependents:

Documents

  • A copy of your discharge or separation papers (DD214)
  • Copies of relevant medical records

Personal information

  • Your social Security number
  • Direct deposit information

Information about your dependents

  • Dates of birth
  • Social Security numbers

We can help you find out what documents to bring to your visit. We can also request documents for you. Call the office for more information.
