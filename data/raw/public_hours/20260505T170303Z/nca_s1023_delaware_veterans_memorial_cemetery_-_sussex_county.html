Office Hours

Monday thru Friday 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.
Closed on all National or State Recognized Holidays

Visitation Hours

The Cemetery is open to visitors every day of the year from dawn until dusk.

Burial Space

The Delaware Veterans Memorial Cemetery offers ample space to accommodate both casketed and cremated remains. We provide a serene and respectful environment to honor and remember those who have served.

General Information

A grave locator kiosk is available at the administration building to assist visitors in finding specific burial locations and obtaining general information.

* General Information and grave locator kiosk available at the administration building
