Hyannis VA Clinic

Every patient has a Primary Care Provider who will coordinate all your healthcare needs. He/she will make sure you receive appropriate care by providing easy access to any of the Providence VA Medical Center's 32 Subspecialty Clinics. For more information on how to enroll in the Hyannis Community-Based Outpatient Clinic (CBOC) Primary Care Center, please contact the Patient Services Department at 866-363-4486 Ext. 13300.
