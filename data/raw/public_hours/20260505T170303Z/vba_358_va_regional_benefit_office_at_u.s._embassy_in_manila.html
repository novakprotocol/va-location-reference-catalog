Prepare for your visit

Select a topic to learn more.

Appointments are required; walk-ins cannot be accommodated. 

Visitors seeking assistance from the Public Contact Team at the Manila Regional Office are encouraged to schedule a virtual or in-person appointment on the Visitor Engagement Reporting Application (VERA). VERA is a self-service portal which allows visitors to schedule, reschedule, and cancel appointments.

Please select the VERA link for Manila in-person appointment or virtual appointment link. You will receive email confirmation of your appointment.

Note:  Laptop computers, recording devices, smart watches, and/or any large electronic devices are not allowed inside the VA Manila Regional Office and Outpatient Clinic.

When you visit, please bring a current, unexpired photo ID. Depending on the reason for your visit, you may need to bring additional these documents and information about you and your dependents:

  • Documents
    • A copy of your discharge or separation papers (DD214)
    • Copies of relevant medical records
  • Personal information
    • Your social Security number
    • Direct deposit information
  • Information about your dependents
    • Dates of birth
    • Social Security numbers  

 

Important to note:  Laptop computers, recording devices, smart watches, and/or any large electronic devices are not allowed inside the VA Manila Regional Office and Outpatient Clinic.

How to get to the VA Regional Office and Outpatient Clinic (VARO & OPC) view the Manila map.

The VA Regional Office and Outpatient Clinic is on the U.S. Embassy Seafront Compound in Pasay City,  located along Roxas Blvd next to Cuneta Astrodome, between Gil Puyat (aka Buendia) and EDSA.

Get to EDSA and Roxas Blvd, go west on Roxas and a few meters after Embassy of Japan, turn right to US Embassy Seafront Compound Service Road. The VA Building is the left structure facing the US Embassy gate (photo on the top of this page).

Designated parking area is available in front of the VA building, along the service road, on a limited basis.  Ensure that vehicle pass is clearly posted on the vehicle dashboard to avoid towing.

Nearest pay parking is in front of the Cuneta Astrodome, or about 300m next to the Embassy of Japan.  Park at your own risk, and make sure to get a parking stub before leaving the car.
