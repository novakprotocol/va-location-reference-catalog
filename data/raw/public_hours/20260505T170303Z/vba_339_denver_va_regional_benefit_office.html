Prepare for your visit

Select a topic to learn more.

Please park in the south lot directly in front of the building entrance. Overflow parking is on the hill above, with steps leading down. Handicap parking is available in front of the building entrance. 

Visitors seeking assistance from the Public Contact Team at the Denver Regional Office are encouraged to schedule a virtual or in-person appointment on the Visitor Engagement Reporting Application (VERA). VERA is a self-service portal which allows visitors to schedule, reschedule, and cancel appointments.

To schedule an appointment, please visit VERA. You will receive email confirmation of your appointment.

Our office is 0.7 miles from the W Line light rail stop at Federal Center. For more details, visit the Denver Regional Transportation District map and schedule.

When you visit, please bring a current, unexpired photo ID. Depending on the reason for your visit, you may need to bring additional these documents and information about you and your dependents: 

Documents 

  • A copy of your discharge or separation papers (DD214)  
  • Copies of relevant medical records  

Personal information  

  • Your social Security number  
  • Direct deposit information  

Information about your dependents  

  • Dates of birth  
  • Social Security numbers  

We can help you find out what documents to bring to your visit. We can also request documents for you. Call the office for more information.
