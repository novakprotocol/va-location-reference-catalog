Wilmington VA Mobile Medical Unit

Our mobile clinic offers primary care and other services to Veterans who are unable to visit our medical center or one of our clinics. Our Wilmington VA Mobile Medical Unit attends community outreach events and has assisted in vaccinating thousands of Veterans against COVID-19.
