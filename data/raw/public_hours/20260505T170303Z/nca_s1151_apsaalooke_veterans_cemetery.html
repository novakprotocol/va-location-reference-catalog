The Crow Constitution and
​By-Laws of 2001 established three branches of government, the Executive, Legislative, and Judicial branches. The governing body of the Crow Tribe exercises a separation and balance of power through these three branches, as stated in ARTICLE I of the Constitution and By-Laws.
