From the north

To arrive at the VA medical center from the north, travel south on Interstate 29 taking exit 79 heading east.  Next, turn south on Kiwanis, then east on 22nd Street.  The VA medical center is located at 2501 West 22nd Street.

From the south

To arrive at the VA medical center from the south, travel north on I-29 taking exit 9 heading east.  Next, turn north on Kiwanis, then turn east on 22nd Street.  The VA medical center is located at  2501 West 22nd Street.

From the east

To arrive at the VA medical center from the east, travel west on I-90 turning south on I-29.  Next, take exit 79 heading east and turn south on Kiwanis.  You will next turn east on 22nd Street.  The VA medical center is located at 2501 West 22nd Street.

From the west

To arrive at the VA medical center from the west, travel east on I-90, turning south on I-29. Next, take exit 79 heading east, then turn south on Kiwanis and, finally, turn east on 22nd Street.  The VA medical center is located at 2501 West 22nd Street.

The VA Sioux Falls health care adaptive sports program is intended to provide structured programming for Veterans with physical or mental health challenges that limit their independent participation in sports.

A multidisciplinary approach is used to provide Adaptive Sports evaluations and treatments. Adaptive Sports events include, but are not limited to, Ski for Light, National Veterans Wheelchair Games, National Disabled Veterans Winter Sports Clinic, National Veteran Golden Age Games, National Disabled Veterans Golf Clinic, National Veterans Summer Sports Clinic, and the National Veterans Creative Arts Festival. The purpose of programming is to facilitate Veteran health, independence, well-being, and quality of life through adaptive sports engagement.

Veterans must be able to independently complete activities of daily living (ADL) or bring an approved caregiver to assist with ADLs. Caregivers’ assistance approval must be included in the Adaptive Sports consult describing how their participation is integral for Veteran’s successfully participation.

Veterans are responsible for transportation to and from local adaptive sports events.

Our palliative and hospice care services support you and your family as you cope with a life-threatening or terminal illness. Palliative care works to provide comfort, reduce suffering, and improve the quality of life for patients being treated for serious illnesses. Hospice care focuses on helping patients with terminal illnesses to manage the final stages of their lives. Our palliative and hospice care services include:

  • Pain and symptom management
  • Emotional, spiritual, and psychosocial support for you and your family
  • Help with practical issues that include an advance directive (living will), health care power of attorney, funeral arrangements, and applying for VA death and burial benefits
  • Education to help you and your family make informed health care decisions and know what to expect as your health declines
  • Bereavement care to help surviving family members during the grieving process

Post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) is a mental health problem that some people develop after experiencing or witnessing a life-threatening event, like combat, a natural disaster, a car accident, or sexual assault. It’s normal to have upsetting memories, feel on edge, or have trouble sleeping after this type of event. If symptoms last more than a few months, it may be PTSD. We can help you with effective treatments and support services, like:

  • Psychiatric and psychological counseling
  • Services for Veterans who are homeless
  • Treatment for addictive behaviors and substance use
  • Transition and care management for post-9/11 combat Veterans

Whole health

Whole health puts you at the center of care, rather than your illnesses or conditions. We work with you to develop a personalized health plan based on your values, needs, and goals.

Appointments

Main Phone

Main Phone

Whole Health is care that empowers and equips you to take charge of your health and well-being so that you can live life to its fullest. It is care that goes beyond your disease and illness to focus on your values and goals. In a Whole Health approach to care you will start hearing providers ask, "what matters most to you" or "what do you want your health for"? Whole Health includes self-care strategies and integrative health therapies. Our Integrative Health Clinic offers services:

  • Acupuncture and massage therapy
  • Aromatherapy
  • Health Coaching
  • Meditation and mindfulness training, guided imagery, relaxation
  • Mind-Body Medicine
  • Yoga and Tai Chi classes
  • 6 week Whole health skills building course
  • Biofeedback
