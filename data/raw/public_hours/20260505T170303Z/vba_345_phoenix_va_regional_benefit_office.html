Prepare for your visit

Select a topic to learn more.

Visitors seeking assistance from the Public Contact Team at the Phoenix Regional Office are encouraged to schedule a virtual or in-person appointment on the Visitor Engagement Reporting Application (VERA). VERA is a self-service portal which allows visitors to schedule, reschedule, and cancel appointments. You will receive email confirmation of your appointment.

Services are available on a walk-in from 8:00 am to 4:00 pm or by appointment Monday thru Friday. 

Additionally, if you are unable to attend an in person appointment, there are virtual appointments available as well.

If you schedule a virtual appointment, one of our representatives will reach out to you via your contact information that you provide and the specified date and time of your choosing.

Free parking is available in the visitor parking lot directly in front of the building entrance. 

All visitors are subject to security screening, this includes scanning by metal detector.  

Phoenix VA Regional Office is across the street from the Osborn/Central Avenue station. Maps and schedules can be found on Valley Metro's website Central Phoenix.

When you visit, please bring a current, unexpired photo ID. Depending on the reason for your visit, you may need to bring additional these documents and information about you and your dependents:  

 Documents  

  • A copy of your discharge or separation papers (DD214) 
  • Copies of relevant medical records   

Personal information   

  • Your social Security number 
  • Direct deposit information   

Information about your dependents   

  • Dates of birth 
  • Social Security numbers   

 We can help you find out what documents to bring to your visit. We can also request documents for you. Call the office for more information.
