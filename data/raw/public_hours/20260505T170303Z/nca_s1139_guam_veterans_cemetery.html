About Us

Guam Veterans Cemetery is the final resting place for more than 5,000 active duty service members, veterans and their families. This cemetery bears witness to the military service and sacrifice of men and women in uniform.

We believe that caring for our island’s heroes and their families during their time of need is a sacred duty entrusted to us. Our island’s veterans are laid to rest with dignity and honor at Guam Veterans Cemetery by a compassionate and dedicated work force.

Our Services

Cremation

Cremation services are offered through funeral homes. The Guam Veterans Affairs Office provides the crematory plot.

Transporting Remains

When finding a special place for your loved one, you might recognize transporting their remains is necessary. This is offered from the airport, hospital, and funeral homes.

Receiving Remains

The Guam Veterans Affairs Office can have the authorization to receive remains provided proper documentation is processed.

Direct Burial

The Guam Veterans Affairs Office provides direct burial services for your loved ones.

Funeral

The Guam Veterans Affairs Office offers funeral services for all religions. Learn more about funeral services in the link above.

Graveside Service

With the graveside services provided, we ensure that your loved ones are laid to rest with the 21 Gun Salute, an honorable pastor, or a funeral director.

Arrangements

 The arrangements you make will reflect your loved ones exact wishes and desires. Arranging their services will help ease the burden and alleviate any questions, problems or differences.

We’d be pleased to meet with you to discuss further.

What Our Families are Saying

We always visit the cemetery on weekends and we appreciate the cleanliness and upkeep of the grounds and columbariums. It means a lot to our family that the resting place of our brother is respected.

Visiting my sisters at the Guam Veterans Cemetery is a routine our family keeps up with every year. We are glad to see that the grounds are maintained with such pristine.

My grandfather would’ve been happy to see that where he lies is always kept up with. We aren’t always around to keep up his resting place, but it seems there are hardworking individuals that make sure all resting places are well kept and respected.
