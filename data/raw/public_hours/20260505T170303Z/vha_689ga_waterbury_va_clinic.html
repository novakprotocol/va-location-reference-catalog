Local transportation services

Beneficiary Travel

Beneficiary travel benefits include round-trip transportation from your home to the medical center, mileage reimbursement, or special mode transport. 

You can also contact your local Beneficiary Travel Department at /7498/6615 for more information.

Not Eligible for Beneficiary Travel?

If you’re not eligible for Beneficiary Travel but need a ride to an appointment, we may be able to help using our local Van Service or shuttles. Please contact us at /7498/6615 for more information.
