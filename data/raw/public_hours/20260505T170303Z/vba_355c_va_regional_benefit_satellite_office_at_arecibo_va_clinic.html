San Juan VA Regional Benefit Office

We help Veterans, service members, and their families access VA benefits and services. Benefits we can help with include disability compensation, education benefits, life insurance, pensions, and home loans.

VA Regional Office San Juan 

50 CARR 165 Guaynabo, Puerto Rico 00968-8024 

FROM AIRPORT/CAROLINA: When exiting the airport follow the signs to San Juan taking Ave. Baldorioty de Castro/PR-26 Oeste - San Juan, Isla Verde. Driving approximately 4 miles from the airport, take the exit to PR-22 Hato Rey, Bayamón, Caguas. You will go through a short tunnel. Keep going straight and follow signs to PR-18 Sur, Río Piedras, Caguas, Ponce. Take exit into Expreso José de Diego/PR-22 Oeste. Drive approximately 2 minutes and take exit 7B, onto PR 165/PR-24, Buchanan-San Patricio. Pass a traffic light; the Regional Office building will be on your right side at the second traffic light. (Travel time: approximately 9 miles, 12 minutes from the airport). 

FROM BAYAMON (Expreso José De Diego): When exiting the Buchanan Toll, drive approximately 1 mile and take the first exit to the right, 4B. Pass two traffic lights; the Regional Office building will be on your right side at the second traffic light. 

FROM CAGUAS (Expresso Luis A. Ferré): When exiting Expreso Luis A. Ferré into PR 18 drive approximately 5 miles and take exit to PR-22, Bayamón Expreso José de Diego/PR-22. Drive approximately 2 minutes and take exit 7B, onto PR 165/PR-24, Buchanan San Patricio. Pass a traffic light; the Regional Office building will be on your right side at the second traffic light. 

When you visit, please bring a current, unexpired photo ID.    

Depending on the reason for your visit, you may need to bring additional documents and information about you and your dependents, including

Documents

  • A copy of your discharge or separation papers (DD214)
  • Copies of relevant medical records

Personal information

  • Your Social Security number 
  • Direct deposit information

Information about your dependents

  • Dates of birth 
  • Social Security numbers

We can help you find out what documents to bring to your visit. We can also request documents for you. Call the office for more information.
