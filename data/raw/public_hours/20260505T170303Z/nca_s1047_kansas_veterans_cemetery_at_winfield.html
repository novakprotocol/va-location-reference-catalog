Veteran Cemeteries

Kansas Veterans' Cemetery at Winfield

Hours of Operation

Office hours are Monday through Friday 7:30 am to 4:00 pm.

Office closed on State holidays except for Memorial Day ceremony.

Visitation Hours

The Kansas Veterans’ Cemetery at Winfield is open to visitors daily, from sunrise to sunset. Floral regulations for the cemetery are posted on the cemetery grounds.

Cemetery Location

1208 North College Street
Winfield, Kansas  67156

The cemetery is located in Winfield, Kansas just off of College Street near the Kansas Veterans' Home.
