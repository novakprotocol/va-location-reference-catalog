Some services are available on a walk-in basis, but we encourage you to use our self-service portal to schedule a virtual or in-person appointment. 

Visit Visitor Engagement Reporting Application (VERA) to schedule, reschedule, or cancel appointments.

Parking is free in the on-site parking garage. 

Turn right onto Southern Lane. Turn right at the stop sign and then turn left to enter the visitor's section in the parking garage. Drive toward the gate and press the intercom to speak with security for access. 

The Isakson VA Atlanta Regional Office can be reached via MARTA bus route 19. 

Visit MARTA for routes and schedules

When you visit, please bring a current, unexpired photo ID.    

Depending on the reason for your visit, you may need to bring additional documents and information about you and your dependents, including:

Documents

  • A copy of your discharge or separation papers (DD214)
  • Copies of relevant medical records

Personal information

  • Your Social Security number 
  • Direct deposit information

Information about your dependents

  • Dates of birth 
  • Social Security numbers

We can help you find out what documents to bring to your visit. We can also request documents for you.  Call 1- for more information.
