State Veterans Cemeteries

The Tennessee Department of Veterans Services maintains and manages five Veterans Cemeteries in the state to include one in Nashville, one in Memphis, two in Knoxville and one in Parkers Crossroads.  To pre-register for burial, please click on the cemetery of your choice.

The Middle Tennessee State Veterans Cemetery was established in January 1993. This cemetery has the capacity for 30,813 plots.

Middle Tennessee State Veterans Cemetery
7931 McCrory Lane
Nashville, TN 37221
(615) 532-2238
Hours of operation:  Monday-Friday 8:00 a.m. -4:30 p.m. (CT)
MTSV.Cemetery@tn.gov

Pre-Registration
Veterans and dependents can pre-register for burial in the Tennessee State Veterans Cemetery. Click on the Pre-Register for Burial icon below, fill out the pertinent information, click on the submit button, then mail the Veteran's DD-214 or NG Form 22 (discharge papers) to the Middle Tennessee State Veterans Cemetery. For spouse burial requests, please also include marriage certificate. You will receive a formal response from the Tennessee Department of Veterans Services in the mail 4 to 6 weeks from the date the application is received. All applications are subject to eligibility verification, review and approval.

Pre-Registration Form
Please complete this form to pre-register for interment at the Middle TN State Veterans Cemetery. Your application will be submitted for verification and review. After your application has been submitted, please mail the Veteran's DD-214 or NG Form 22 (discharge papers) to the cemetery. For spouse burial requests, please include marriage certificate. Your application will not be processed until discharge papers have been received.

The Tennessee State Veterans Cemetery at Parkers Crossroads was established in July 2018. This cemetery has a capacity of 49,000 plots.

Tennessee State Veterans Cemetery at Parkers Crossroads
693 Wildersville Road
Parkers Crossroads, TN 38388
(731) 967-4127
TSVCPC.Cemetery@tn.gov

Pre-Registration
Veterans and dependents can pre-register for burial in the Tennessee State Veterans Cemetery at Parkers Crossroads . Click on the Pre-Register for Burial icon below, fill out the pertinent information, upload your verification documents and click on the submit button.  If you are unable to upload your documents, please mail the Veteran's DD-214 or NG Form 22 (discharge papers) to the Tennessee State Veterans Cemetery at Parkers Crossroads. For spouse burial requests, please also include marriage certificate. Your application will not be processed until discharge papers have been received.  You will receive a formal response from the Tennessee Department of Veterans Services in the mail 4 to 6 weeks from the date the application is received.  All applications are subject to eligibility verification, review and approval.

Pre-Registration Form
Please complete the secure online form to pre-register for interment at the Tennessee State Veterans Cemetery at Parkers Crossroads. Your application will be submitted for verification and review.  After your application has been submitted, if you were unable to upload your discharge documents, please mail the Veteran's DD-214 or NG Form 22 (discharge papers) to the cemetery. For spouse burial requests, please include marriage certificate. Your application will not be processed until discharge papers have been received.
