Hours of Operation and Information

The Southwest Virginia Veterans Cemetery is open to the public for visitation everyday during daylight hours. The cemetery administrative office is open Monday through Friday from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., except for federal and state holidays. The cemetery administrative office may be reached at 855-4VA-VETS (855-482-8387) during business hours.

After hours calls will be returned the next business day.

Funeral directors should call 855-4VA-VETS (855-482-8387) during normal business hours to schedule interment services. If a cemetery team member does not answer, please leave a message – your call will be returned no later than the next business day. The cemetery staff looks forward to visiting with and assisting each family.

Please read our cemetery filming and photography policy.

Provisions for Pre-application

For expediency, the veteran may apply prior to death to determine eligibility for interment in the cemetery.

To download a copy of the pre-application form, please click the link below. You may also request a copy of the pre-application by calling the cemetery at 855-4VA-VETS (855-482-8387).

Please remember that in addition to the pre-application form, you must submit documentation of military service (DD214 or equivalent) and a copy of a marriage certificate, if applicable. You must also inform cemetery management of other dependent eligibility status at time of application.

Pre-Application Form

Casket Outer Burial Receptacle/Crypts

  • Outer burial receptacles are required for in-ground casketed burials at the three state veteran cemeteries. An outer burial receptacle is an enclosure made of reinforced concrete, steel, or polyurethane.
  • No Cost: USDVA-provided outer burial receptacles are provided at no cost to the veteran/eligible dependents. These are the same units used at Arlington/Quantico National Cemeteries.
  • Families not wishing to use the outer burial receptacle provided by the cemetery at no cost should contact the cemetery to discuss other options.

Events

Memorial Day Events

To commemorate Memorial Day, flags will be placed on all grave sites and the Avenue of Flags will be erected along the circle drive. A public ceremony will be held in honor of the U.S. military personnel who died while serving in the United States Armed Forces.

The administration office will be open from 9 a.m. until 3 p.m. to assist veterans and their families with grave site location, general information, and filling out the cemetery pre-application form.

Read more about the 2025 Memorial Day ceremonies.

Veterans Day Events

The cemetery will hold an open house event in honor of Veterans Day (November 11). Employees will be available from 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. to answer questions about pre-applications, burial benefits in one of Virginia’s state-operated veterans cemeteries and cemetery operating procedures. Employees will also be available to assist visitors with locating grave sites.

The cemetery will be in full dress with the Avenue of Flags flying and individual flags on each grave site.

Annual Wreath Laying

Every December, holiday wreaths are placed on each gravesite in accordance with the National Wreath Laying Day. Please visit swvavcv.org for updates concerning this public event. 

Learn more about the Wreath Laying Ceremony on December 13, 2025.
