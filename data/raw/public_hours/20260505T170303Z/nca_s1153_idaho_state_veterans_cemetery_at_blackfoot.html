Idaho State Veterans Cemetery – Blackfoot

Overview

The 40-acre Idaho State Veterans Cemetery – Blackfoot serves more than 20,000 eastern Idaho veterans and their families. It has plots for in-ground casket burials and three options for cremation. There is space for in-ground inurnment, a scatter garden and a columbarium, which is a wall with niches to place urns.
