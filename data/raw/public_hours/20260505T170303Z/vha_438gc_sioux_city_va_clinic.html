Military sexual trauma, or MST, is the term VA uses to refer to sexual assault or sexual harassment that occurred during military service. For some MST survivors, the experience affects their physical and mental health, work, relationships and everyday life, even many years later. We offer care for physical and mental health conditions related to MST throughout our various clinics. For MST-related mental health concerns, we offer individual and group counseling, psychiatric care,  and walk-in care.

Post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) is a mental health problem that some people develop after experiencing or witnessing a life-threatening event, like combat, a natural disaster, a car accident, or sexual assault. It’s normal to have upsetting memories, feel on edge, or have trouble sleeping after this type of event. If symptoms last more than a few months, it may be PTSD. We can help you with effective treatments and support services, like:

  • Psychiatric and psychological counseling
  • Services for Veterans who are homeless
  • Treatment for addictive behaviors and substance use
  • Transition and care management for post-9/11 combat Veterans

Whole health

Whole health puts you at the center of care, rather than your illnesses or conditions. We work with you to develop a personalized health plan based on your values, needs, and goals.

Appointments

Main Phone

Main Phone

Whole Health is care that empowers and equips you to take charge of your health and well-being so that you can live life to its fullest. It is care that goes beyond your disease and illness to focus on your values and goals. In a Whole Health approach to care you will start hearing providers ask, "what matters most to you" or "what do you want your health for"? Whole Health includes self-care strategies and integrative health therapies. Our Integrative Health Clinic offers services:

  • Acupuncture and massage therapy
  • Aromatherapy
  • Health Coaching
  • Meditation and mindfulness training, guided imagery, relaxation
  • Mind-Body Medicine
  • Yoga and Tai Chi classes
  • 6 week Whole health skills building course
  • Biofeedback
